Our story

We are a fellowship of women growing in Christ.

Wogifh began with a simple desire: to help women discover their divine purpose, assist in harnessing their god given potentials and ensuring the less privileged and vulnerable are reached with love and care.

Our invitation

Bring your love and your hunger for god. Bring your love for God and His people. There is room for all of it here.

The way we walk

Rooted.
Ready. Bold.

we are a fellowship of women pursuing spiritual growth, deep friendship, and kingdom purpose. We read, reflect, pray, and encourage one another as we learn to live faithfully for Christ.

Our gatherings are shaped by grace, wisdom, and intentional community. Every woman is invited to come as she is and grow into the woman God has called her to be.
